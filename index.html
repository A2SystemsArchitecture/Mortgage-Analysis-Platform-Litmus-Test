Why HomeTrue exists

The gap between what you're quoted
and what you actually pay is real.

My son and his wife were buying their first home in Miami-Dade. They kept getting estimates from realtors and lenders that looked manageable — until they weren't.

The gap wasn't dishonesty. It was omission. Property taxes reassessed at purchase price. Flood insurance. HOA fees buried in the fine print. PMI that nobody mentioned. Every estimate was technically accurate and completely misleading at the same time.

So I built them a tool. You put in the property, the price, your income, your credit score. The tool runs best case and worst case — mortgage, taxes, HOA, insurance, PMI, flood. It tells you what it costs to close. It walks you through the Homestead Exemption filing step by step.

No broker. No agenda. Just honest numbers.
