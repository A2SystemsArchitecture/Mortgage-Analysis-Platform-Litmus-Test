## November 21 deeper source trace: official Lumpkin 2026 tax information flyer

- Official county Tax Commissioner's 2026 two-page brochure: https://www.lumpkincounty.gov/DocumentCenter/View/3147/2026-Tax-Information-Flyer , linked directly from county government's Tax Commissioner page https://www.lumpkincounty.gov/288/Tax-Commissioner . Both PDF pages were visually inspected in a page screenshot. It does **not publish the final 2026 millage schedule** and is not substituted for a signed rate.
- Page 2 states ordinary taxable property assessed at **40%** of value. Standard homestead exemption **$2,000 deducted from 40% assessed value** for the county/county school M&O portion and state rate, **not bonded indebtedness** (levy-specific applicability must be checked before applying).
- Page 2 local Lumpkin exemptions include: age **62+** with federal adjusted gross income at most **$20,000**, **$30,000 of assessed value for school taxes**; age **65+** or permanently disabled with stated qualifications, up to **$120,000 of fair-market value for school tax**; separate county/city relief up to **$60,000 of assessed value** for qualifying age 65+ or permanently disabled; age **62+** and net annual income at most **$10,000**, an increased elderly school exemption up to **$10,000** deducted from assessed value; elderly general exemption age **65+** and income at most **$10,000** up to **$4,000 assessed value** across county, school, state subject to bond levy exclusions; additional relief described for veterans, survivors and eligible other classifications. The flyer explains qualification restrictions and exclusions, and special exemptions are not automatically stacked. Do **not** translate these to automatic household eligibility without the Tax Commissioner's confirmation.
