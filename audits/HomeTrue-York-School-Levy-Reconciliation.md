# York legal-residence levy reconciliation

The [City of York affordability study](https://www.yorksc.gov/media/9431), finalized February 16, 2026, applies the full aggregate rate in a $299,995 owner-occupied illustration, producing a rounded $6,076. Its component decimals are interpreted as rates per dollar because that is how the study calculates the example, despite its inconsistent unit wording.

[S.C. Code §12-37-220(B)(47)](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t12c037) excludes school operating taxes for approved legal residences and retains general-obligation debt millage. In the study's hypothetical inputs, the 231.8 school operating mills are excluded, while 110.7 city, 70.9 county and 93.0 school debt mills remain. $299,995 × 4% × 0.2746 = $3,295.15 before any other credits or fees, rounded to cents. HomeTrue's standalone South Carolina function returned the same amount.

The study is a fiscal-year affordability illustration, not a verified current parcel tax bill or final levy schedule. City of York is not one of HomeTrue's current York district choices. Do not apply those rates to Rock Hill, Fort Mill or unincorporated York. The market guidance now states the verified exemption boundary; the district planning rates and full market sign-off remain open.
