# Cobb County local reconciliation

The [Cobb County School District budget](https://media.cobbk12.org/media/WWWCobb/medialib/fy2026-budget-document.3cef95127772.pdf) describes the $10,000 basic homestead deduction for eligible homeowners in the county general and school general categories. The [Tax Commissioner estimator](https://gis.cobbcounty.org/taxestimator/) instructs homeowners to apply before the assessment notice appeal deadline, states that the estimator uses 2025 adopted rates, and excludes city taxes. The draft county guidance now reflects this evidence. The standalone Georgia scenario continues to model the statewide standard exemption, with the Cobb deduction outside that scenario.

The [Georgia DOR 2025 digest](https://dor.georgia.gov/document/document/2025-georgia-county-ad-valorem-tax-digest-millage-ratespdf/download) lists 8.460 unincorporated county, 2.970 fire, and 18.700 school mills. For the ordinary unincorporated scenario without special district levies or exemptions, 30.130 mills times $120,000 assessed value on a $300,000 home gives $3,615.60 before fees. The standalone Georgia scenario matched this benchmark. The existing 0.68% planning input yields $2,040 annually before rounding, a $1,575.60 difference against this historical scenario. This is a historical comparison, not a verified current bill.

The [2026 county hearing notice](https://www.cobbcounty.gov/news/2027-budget-millage-information-updates) and [school notice](https://www.cobbk12.org/notice-of-property-tax-increase.131370) are pre-adoption evidence. The final 2026 rate chart could not be verified from the Tax Commissioner rate page linked by the estimator. No 2026 district rate was substituted. Marietta, Smyrna, and Kennesaw still require current separately billed levies where applicable, parcel-specific overlays, eligibility and buyer-total verification. No sign-off or deployment.

## Follow-up on 2026 adoption

- County general (8.460), fire (2.970), and Cumberland SSD II (2.450): the [county notice](https://www.cobbcounty.gov/news/press-release-announcing-proposed-property-tax-increase) gives these figures as proposed, and the [county meeting calendar](https://www.cobbcounty.gov/events/2026-07-28board-commissioners-regular-meeting) and [official meeting recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wlFmcejWzu4) identify the July 28 meeting. The [third-party meeting summary](https://www.openpublica.com/meetings/cobbcounty-georgia-board-of-commissioners-2026-07-28-6a6a1c4755dd56a3321c56da) reports that the three figures passed. The signed levy or equivalent primary evidence of the final vote was not verified.
- Cobb school general (18.700): the [school notice](https://www.cobbk12.org/notice-of-property-tax-increase.131370) identifies a tentative rate. The final 2026 school levy was not verified.
- The [official tax estimator](https://gis.cobbcounty.org/taxestimator/) explicitly uses 2025 adopted rates and excludes city charges. Its linked current-rate chart was inaccessible during verification. The 2023 [Cobb Tax Commissioner chart](https://www.cobbtax.org/Property/Millage%20Rates/2023%20MILLAGE%20RATES.pdf) demonstrates why a single countywide rate is insufficient, but its district breakdown is historical.
- All four HomeTrue district values remain planning inputs. Unincorporated Cobb needs final county and school evidence plus district applicability. Smyrna and Kennesaw also need adopted city levies and applicable fire and special-district treatment. Marietta needs its own adopted city and independent school levies and applicable overlays. The figures above remain a source trail, not replacement inputs.

The standalone local Cobb basic-homestead calculator is implemented and tested at commit 1c5a68a. This follow-up concerns rates used by the main report. No district rates were changed.
