Interpretation and release decision

The stored percentage is a HomeTrue effective-rate assumption, not necessarily a legislated tax rate for a particular parcel. County, city, school, fire, debt and special levies, assessment ratios, exemptions and eligibility must be reconciled before certifying a 2027 customer estimate.

Approved standard: Use enacted 2027 laws, latest confirmed local rates where 2027 rates are not adopted, label the source tax year, and keep the estimation notice visible until each applicable input is confirmed. January 1 does not automatically certify all local 2027 rates.

No production deployment authorized. Review branch PR #2.

Verified official reference examples

North Carolina: NCDOR publishes fiscal 2026–27 rates. Durham County officially reports countywide $0.5792 per $100 and City of Durham $1.0163 combined per $100 for fiscal 2026–27. These are separate jurisdictions, not one Durham County percentage.

Guilford County: adopted fiscal 2026–27 countywide rate $0.7895 per $100 assessed value. Mecklenburg County: adopted fiscal 2026–27 county rate $0.4927 per $100; municipality levies are additional. County official tax-rate schedule. Wake County: 2026 tax bills are based on rates adopted June 2026 and may include fire-district charges. Official tax-rate source. Official adoption notice. Other local levies must be added where applicable.

Florida: 2027 approved exemption changes do not exempt school taxes uniformly. Florida DOR November 2026 bulletin. Texas: $140,000 school homestead exemption applies to school districts. Texas Comptroller.

Critical deficiencies not solved by changing a year label

Florida's exemptions require school/non-school rate breakdown; Texas needs the school levy identified; Indiana has payable-year deductions; Georgia's exemption is based on assessed value; South Carolina legal-residence and school-operating relief must be separated. All are presently inadequately represented by the platform's single blended-rate calculation.

31 markets have zero-valued baseline flood estimate fields. Zero in the database means no reliable baseline entered, not that a buyer owes $0 for flood coverage.
