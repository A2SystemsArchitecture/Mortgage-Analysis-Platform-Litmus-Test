# Batch 1 state homestead checkpoint

Review draft only. State sign-off remains open for Florida, Georgia, Alabama and South Carolina.

| State | Reconciled in the state guide | Official authority |
|---|---|---|
| Florida | Added separately labeled 2026 school and non-school exemption components, kept the existing 2027 transition, and labeled the returned county percentage as a purchase-price planning rate. For a qualifying home assessed at $100,000 in 2026, the school taxable base is $75,000 and the non-school base is $48,589 before other relief or special assessments. These are independently derived benchmarks for future calculator reconciliation. | [2026 statute](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/196.031), [2026 CPI adjustment](https://floridarevenue.com/property/Documents/cpi_homestead_exemption.pdf), [2027 bulletin](https://floridarevenue.com/property/Documents/PTO_BUL_26-21.pdf) |
| Georgia | Removed the unsupported assumption that most counties add a larger exemption. The ordinary 40% assessment is separate from the $2,000 standard exemption on eligible levies. A $300,000 fair market value corresponds to $120,000 assessed and $118,000 taxable for a levy to which the full standard exemption applies, before other applicable relief. The 2026 grant and 2027 floating exemption are distinct. | [Georgia DOR homestead](https://dor.georgia.gov/property-tax-homestead-exemptions), [2026 DOR legislative update](https://dor.georgia.gov/document/document-document/caveat-2026-opening-session/download) |
| Alabama | Corrected the $300,000 example. The $30,000 assessed value precedes H-1. At the full H-1 amounts, the illustrative bases are $26,000 for state and $28,000 for applicable county taxes before other relief. | [Alabama DOR assessment](https://www.revenue.alabama.gov/property-tax/property-tax-assessment/), [Alabama DOR homestead](https://www.revenue.alabama.gov/property-tax/homestead-exemptions/) |
| South Carolina | Clarified that eligible legal residence relief applies to school operating millage and school debt millage can still apply. Removed an ambiguous repeated $50,000 reference; the guide keeps the regular qualifying homestead exemption and flags the separate temporary 2026 relief for county eligibility confirmation. For a $300,000 fair market value, a 4% ratio yields $12,000 assessed and a 6% ratio yields $18,000 assessed before exemptions. | [South Carolina DOR exemptions](https://www.dor.sc.gov/property/property-tax-exemptions), [South Carolina DOR tax manual](https://dor.sc.gov/sites/dor/files/Documents/Policy%20Manuals/SCTIED-2025%20Edition-Complete.pdf) |

The full guide function from the updated application rendered in Node and passed 13 assertions across the four state branches. Those assertions check rendered guidance, including the three Alabama example values. The Florida, Georgia and South Carolina examples above were calculated independently and are reference benchmarks, not proven calculator behavior.

The property-tax calculator still uses purchase price and a blended planning rate. It does not use the source-rule-specific taxable bases above in the dollar totals. State-level exemption rules require verification against actual implemented behavior, and market tax rates and local levies still need reconciliation. This checkpoint changes guidance only. No launch sign-off or deployment.
