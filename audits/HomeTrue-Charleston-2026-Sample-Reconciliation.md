The South Carolina scenario now accepts verified annual credits and fees. The regression reproduces the published values and tests display and invalid adjustments. The inputs are manually entered. The release market plan rate and buyer report are unchanged. Other Charleston districts, property-specific qualification, fee and credit policies, and later tax years remain open. No full market or state sign-off.
