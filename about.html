The Truth Before the Offer

Why HomeTrue Exists

HomeTrue began with a personal experience. Watching family members navigate the homebuying process highlighted how difficult it can be to understand the potential costs before making an offer.

The purchase price is only part of the picture. Property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance, homeowners association fees, and possible flood coverage can materially affect the monthly cost of owning a home.

HomeTrue was created to help buyers explore a more complete picture of those potential costs before making important decisions.

Our Philosophy

HomeTrue is built on the principles of education and servant leadership. We believe people deserve clear information and the freedom to decide for themselves.

We do not tell people what to buy, where to live, or which professionals to hire. Our role is to explain financial scenarios and help people understand the numbers.

We measure our success by whether people understand their situation better, not by whether they purchase a home.

Our Independence, Privacy, and Advertising

HomeTrue's analysis is designed to serve the person using it, not parties involved in a potential real estate transaction.

Our policy is not to sell customers' personal information, home-search details, financial inputs, or purchase plans to lenders, real estate agents, advertisers, or lead buyers. We do not sell mortgage leads or accept commissions for referring users to lenders or real estate professionals.

General consumer-brand advertising, such as advertising for everyday products, may be considered in the future. This is different from selling a customer's information or steering their home purchase. Any such advertising must be clearly distinguishable from HomeTrue's educational analysis and must not influence its calculations or recommendations.

We intend to keep advertisers separate from the homebuying information users provide. Service providers needed to operate HomeTrue, such as payment processors, may process information to provide those services. Our privacy disclosures will explain the relevant practices.

What HomeTrue Does

HomeTrue provides educational estimates of potential monthly homeownership costs using available information and stated assumptions. These may include principal and interest, property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance, homeowners association fees, and flood insurance.

Financial scenarios help users understand how changes in assumptions, such as interest rates or down payments, may affect estimated costs. Some inputs or expenses may be unavailable, incomplete, or subject to change.

What HomeTrue Does Not Do

HomeTrue is an educational information platform, not a mortgage lender, mortgage broker, real estate brokerage, financial adviser, law firm, tax adviser, or insurance provider.

We do not originate loans, accept loan applications, approve financing, lock interest rates, or provide binding loan or insurance quotes. We do not direct users toward a particular property, lender, or real estate agent.

HomeTrue's estimates are not guarantees of affordability, eligibility, accuracy, or future expenses. Actual terms and costs may differ. Users should verify important information with appropriate licensed professionals before making financial decisions.
