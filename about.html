About HomeTrue

The Truth Before the Offer

HomeTrue helps prospective buyers understand the potential monthly costs of owning a home before making an offer. Our approach is educational, independent, and centered on the person making the decision.

Our beginning

Why We Exist

HomeTrue grew from watching family members navigate the homebuying process and realizing how difficult it can be to see the full range of potential costs early on.

Our purpose is to help buyers explore a clearer financial picture before committing.

Our values

Education & Servant Leadership

  • Explain the numbers without directing the decision.
  • Respect each buyer's choice of home and professionals.
  • Put understanding ahead of whether a purchase happens.

Success means greater understanding, not a completed home purchase.

Our commitments

Independence, Privacy & Advertising

We do not sell mortgage leads or accept lender or real estate referral commissions.

Our policy is not to sell customers' personal information, home-search details, financial inputs, or purchase plans to outside parties.

General consumer advertising may appear in the future. Advertising must remain separate from HomeTrue's educational analysis and must not influence its calculations.

Service providers, including payment processors, may handle information needed to operate the platform. Our privacy disclosures explain these practices.

Our service

What HomeTrue Does

Provides educational estimates and scenarios for potential monthly homeownership costs, which may include:

  • Mortgage principal and interest
  • Property taxes and homeowners insurance
  • Mortgage insurance, HOA fees, and flood coverage

Results depend on available data, user inputs, and assumptions. Some costs may be missing or change.

Our boundaries

What HomeTrue Does Not Do

  • Make homebuying decisions or recommend specific properties or providers.
  • Originate loans, accept applications, approve financing, or lock rates.
  • Provide binding mortgage or insurance quotes, or legal, tax, or financial advice.

HomeTrue is not a mortgage lender, broker, real estate brokerage, financial adviser, law firm, tax adviser, or insurer.

Important information

Estimates, Not Guarantees

HomeTrue's financial estimates are not guarantees of affordability, loan eligibility, accuracy, or future expenses. Actual rates, terms, and costs may differ. Verify important figures with appropriate licensed professionals before making financial decisions.
